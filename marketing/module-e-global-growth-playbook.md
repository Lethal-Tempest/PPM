# Module E — Global Growth Playbook (Zero-Budget Reach & Leads)

Everything here is **free** and done by you (no code). Ordered by impact-per-hour. Work top to
bottom. Target: maximum global visibility and inbound leads for PPM Cocopeat without ad spend.

> Context: the website now has SEO foundations, a blog, country landing pages, structured data and
> a working lead form. This playbook is how you get those pages **seen** and **linked**.

---

## Tier 1 — Do these first (highest impact, one-time setup)

### 1. Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools (30 min)
- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console): add `ppmcocopeat.com`, verify,
  submit `https://www.ppmcocopeat.com/sitemap.xml`. This is how Google discovers all your new pages.
- [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters): add the site (you can import from GSC),
  submit the same sitemap. Bing also feeds Yahoo and DuckDuckGo — free extra reach.
- In both, use "URL Inspection" / "Submit URL" to push your homepage, blog and market pages for
  faster indexing.

### 2. Google Business Profile (30 min) — huge for a real exporter
- Create/claim [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) for PPM (Patparganj, Delhi).
- Category: "Exporter" / "Manufacturer" / "Garden center supplier". Add photos of product, factory,
  containers, certificates. Add website + WhatsApp.
- This makes you show up in Google Maps and "coco peat supplier near me / in Delhi" searches, and
  earns a free Knowledge Panel. Post updates monthly.

### 3. Free B2B marketplaces — where global buyers actually search (2–3 hrs)
Create full listings (use the descriptions in **Module C §2**). Prioritise:

| Platform | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **IndiaMART** | #1 India B2B, huge inbound buyer volume | Free seller account; add all products + specs |
| **TradeIndia** | Large India B2B directory | Free tier |
| **ExportersIndia** | Export-focused directory | Free listing |
| **Alibaba** (free member) | #1 global B2B search | Free tier limited but indexed; upgrade later if it converts |
| **Go4WorldBusiness** | Global importers, buy-leads | Free tier |
| **TradeKey** | Global B2B | Free tier |
| **eWorldTrade / EC21** | EC21 is strong in **South Korea** | Good for your Korea target |
| **Europages** | Strong in **Netherlands / Spain / EU** | Free basic listing; great for EU markets |

Each listing = a backlink + a new place buyers find you. Use consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
everywhere — it reinforces your Google profile.

### 4. FIEO & council registrations (you likely have RCMC already)
- List your company in the [FIEO](https://www.fieo.org) member directory and Coir Board / export
  promotion directories. These are authoritative backlinks that boost domain trust.

## Tier 2 — Ongoing content & social (compounds over time)

### 5. LinkedIn Company Page + founder posting (free, B2B gold)
- Create a **PPM Cocopeat** company page. Post 2–3x/week: product shots, container loading, crop
  results, "did you know" facts from your blog.
- Repurpose each blog article and FAQ into a LinkedIn post (see Module C). Tag crops/regions.
- Connect with greenhouse buyers, importers and distributors in your target countries. LinkedIn
  search + a short version of the Module C cold email works well as a connection note.

### 6. Publish the blog content everywhere (repurpose, don't rewrite)
- You now have **6 articles** + **6 country pages**. Turn each into:
  - A LinkedIn post + a short reel/video.
  - A Q&A answer on Quora / Reddit (r/hydro, r/gardening) — genuinely helpful, link only when relevant.
  - A guest article pitch to horticulture blogs (earns backlinks).
- Aim to add **1 new blog post/month**. Ideas: "coco peat price guide", "coco coir for cannabis/CEA",
  "coir grow bags vs slabs", country-specific posts. (Ask me and I'll write + wire them in.)

### 7. YouTube channel (free video SEO)
- Short videos: block hydration/expansion (satisfying + useful), factory tour, container loading,
  a "how to use coco peat" clip. Embed them in the matching blog posts.
- Video ranks in both YouTube (2nd biggest search engine) and Google. Put your website + WhatsApp
  in every description.

### 8. Free backlinks (raise domain authority = rank higher everywhere)
- Business directories: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Clutch, Kompass, Yalwa, Cybo.
- Answer sourcing queries on **Help A B2B Writer / Featured / Qwoted** (free HARO alternatives).
- Partner cross-links: any customers, freight forwarders, or coir associations who'll link to you.
- Consistency of NAP across all of these also strengthens local SEO.

## Tier 3 — Multilingual & conversion polish

### 9. Make the multilingual strategy pay off
Your regional language pages are a real advantage — now that hreflang is fixed (Chinese was using an
invalid code and is now `zh`), Google will serve the right language page per region. To maximise it:
- **Localise the blog + market pages** into your key languages (Korean, Dutch, Spanish especially).
  Even 2–3 translated articles per language extends reach a lot. (I can generate these.)
- In each marketplace/directory, list in the local language where possible.
- Build a few **local-language backlinks** (e.g. a Korean or Dutch horticulture forum/directory).

### 10. Convert more of the traffic you get
- **Add the Web3Forms key** (see main README) so form leads land in your inbox reliably — the old
  email-app method silently loses mobile leads.
- **Add a real spec-sheet PDF** — it's your best lead magnet (people trade an email for specs).
- Reply to every enquiry within 24h using the Module C scripts. Speed wins B2B deals.
- Ask happy customers for a testimonial/logo — social proof lifts conversion.

## Tier 4 — Measure & double down (monthly, 30 min)
Using GA4 + Search Console (Module B):
- Which **countries** and **pages** bring traffic? Make more content for what's working.
- Which **search queries** show you in Search Console but you rank #8–20? Improve those pages —
  quickest wins in SEO.
- Which channel drives **leads** (form/WhatsApp)? Put your time there.
- Track: sessions, top countries, key events (`generate_lead`, `whatsapp_click`, `file_download`).

## Why we did NOT move to WordPress
You asked whether WordPress would help. It wouldn't — your current React site is now **faster** and
fully **prerendered** (crawlable static HTML), which is what SEO needs. Migrating would cost weeks,
risk losing the rankings you're about to build, and add hosting/security overhead. Keep what you have;
put the energy into Tiers 1–2 above, which is where the real free growth is.
