## 1. Cold Email Sequences (3 personas × 3 steps)

**Sending rules that keep these out of spam & get replies:**
- Send from a domain address (e.g. `sales@ppmcocopeat.com`), not gmail, once the domain mailbox is live.
- One idea per email. No attachments on email #1 (attachments + cold = spam). Link to the spec sheet instead.
- Space the sequence: Day 0 → Day 3 → Day 7. Stop the sequence the moment they reply.
- Personalise the first line of email #1 — reference their crop, region, or a recent post.
