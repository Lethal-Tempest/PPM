# Outreach Batch 1 — Cold Email Sequences

Ready-to-send cold-outreach package for PPM Cocopeat. Placeholders in `[[brackets]]` are the only things to fill in per contact. Two personas, three emails each, Day 0 / Day 3 / Day 7 cadence.

---

## Sending best practices

- **Send from a domain address**, e.g. `sales@ppmcocopeat.com` — never a gmail address. A domain mailbox with SPF/DKIM/DMARC set up keeps you out of spam and reads as a real business.
- **One idea per email.** Each message makes a single ask; don't stack multiple offers.
- **Personalise the first line of email #1** — reference their crop, region, market, or a recent post/listing. Generic openers kill reply rates.
- **No attachments on email #1.** Cold email + attachment = spam filter. Link to the spec sheet (`ppmcocopeat.com`) instead. Attachments come later, after they reply.
- **Cadence: Day 0 → Day 3 → Day 7.** Space the three emails exactly; don't chase harder.
- **Stop the sequence the moment they reply.** Any human reply ends the automation — switch to a personal, one-to-one conversation.

## Where to find these buyers

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — filter by title (procurement director, purchasing manager, category buyer), industry (farming / horticulture / wholesale), and geography. Best for named decision-makers.
- **EC21 & Gobizkorea** — the primary B2B directories for reaching Korean importers and distributors; search "coco peat", "coir", "hydroponic substrate".
- **Europages** — pan-European supplier/buyer directory; strong for Netherlands, Germany, Spain greenhouse and horticulture trade.
- **Importer directories & customs-import databases** — cross-reference HS code **53050040** to find companies already importing coir, plus their volumes.
- **Trade-show attendee & exhibitor lists** — GreenTech Amsterdam, Horti Asia, Fruit Logistica, and Korean agri expos publish participant lists rich with qualified buyers.
